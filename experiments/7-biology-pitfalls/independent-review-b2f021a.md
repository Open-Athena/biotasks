## Actionable finding

**[P2] Include fitted preprocessing leakage alongside supervised feature selection.** Location: `docs/improvements/README.md`, lines 146–158, particularly lines 150–158 ([pinned lines](https://github.com/Open-Athena/biotasks/blob/b2f021a30166fd782dc569b6c3b3e7095a56b9af/docs/improvements/README.md#L146-L158)). I06 restricts its starting condition and remedy to outcome-associated feature selection. Consequently, a workflow that fits an imputer, scaler, or PCA representation using all samples before cross-validation is outside this entry, even when its evaluation target requires predicting new samples without access to their distribution. I07 addresses related biological entities and I33 addresses model-selection reuse; neither describes this preprocessing condition. The already-selected B08 explicitly discusses this mechanism in **Pitfall 4: leaky preprocessing** (journal/manuscript page 8, beginning with preprocessing dependence on the test set), including standardization, PCA, and other unsupervised transformations. Its conclusion also explains matching available preprocessing information to the prediction setting. Add a conditional opportunity for fitting data-dependent preprocessing on each training partition and applying the fitted transform to held-out samples, or broaden I06 with separate source support and conditions. Preserve valid fixed transformations and explicitly transductive analyses. This is an omission in the scientific catalog, not a claim that I06's existing supervised-selection advice is false. [Primary source: Whalen et al., B08](https://escholarship.org/content/qt6f5210xq/qt6f5210xq.pdf).
