**Nature of this revision (prose only; no executable change).** The grader code, the nine-check inventory and ids, all numerical thresholds (2×10⁻⁴ absolute on score, 1×10⁻² relative on p-values, the 1×10⁻³⁰³ small-tail zone, 1×10⁻⁴ absolute on log2fc), the probe suite and every observable acceptance criterion are preserved exactly; the controller's controls rerun against identical executable content. No scientific test, tolerance change or new evidence was produced by this correction — the edits below fix only misleading explanations in the public addendum and in this rationale.

**Corrections made, per review point.** (1) Rule 10 previously claimed that methodological errors (tie/continuity corrections, per-cluster normalization, wrong grouping, shuffled cells) move `z` "by orders of magnitude" and are rejected. That was a universal claim and is withdrawn: a continuity term is roughly half a rank unit divided by sqrt(n(N−n)(N+1)/12), which for the smaller clusters here (e.g. n≈84 of N=17,041) is on the order of 1e-5, well inside the 2e-4 band. The corrected text states the per-pair absolute rule and explicitly acknowledges that the tolerance accepts all sub-threshold differences whatever their cause. (2) Rule 11's "any genuine tail error … is rejected" is replaced by the per-pair relative rule with no universal rejection claim. (3) Rule 12 no longer presents the 1e-303 zone as a consequence of floating-point underflow or of Benjamini–Hochberg scaling: 1e-303 is a normal double, and m/j scaling need not preserve the zone. It now states the implemented two-sided rule (both values ≤ zone ⇒ equivalent regardless of ratio; exactly one side ≤ zone ⇒ fail) and labels the threshold as an empirical calibration to this reference's serialization behaviour. (4) Rule 14's "two-orders-of-magnitude margin" safety-factor rationale and the "rejected by orders of magnitude" claim are removed; the tolerance stays 1e-4, with the measured basis stated and the calibration limitation acknowledged both publicly (sub-band deviations pass) and here. (5) The significant-digits paragraph now states plainly that only the numeric comparisons enforce precision (no digit counting exists; the reference itself serializes score/log2fc with 2–9 significant digits and p-values with 16–17). (6) Rule 9 is now described as what the grader does — a banded adjacent-increase comparison on both the submitted and the reference score sequences in submitted rank order — and explicitly not as a guarantee against cumulative inversions with sub-band adjacent steps.

**Private calibration numbers (measurements of the three local artifacts — reference, recomputed oracle, independent implementation; none new).** Score: max |Δ| reference-vs-oracle 1.045e-4 (1 row > 1e-4), reference-vs-independent 1.013e-4 (1 row > 1e-4), oracle-vs-independent 6.67e-5 — the single-precision-storage/tie-resolution residual the 2e-4 tolerance covers. log2fc: max |Δ| 1.935e-6 for both faithful pairs; reference text granularity ≈5.94e-8 relative. p-values: max relative difference 1.823e-3 (oracle) / 2.231e-3 (independent) on rows where both sides exceed 1e-303, consistent with |z|·Δz (|z| up to 85.9). Underflow structure: the reference has 1,117 exact zeros in both pval and pval_adj; smallest nonzero pval ≈1.25e-310 and smallest nonzero pval_adj ≈2.2e-308 (subnormal); across the faithful pairwise comparisons there were zero rows with a zero on one side against a >1e-303 value on the other — an empirical observation about these artifacts, not a mathematical property of double precision or of BH. Tie structure: 13,733 exact-score tie groups covering 148,398 rows; maximum rank displacement between the reference and a faithful recomputation is 799 positions, while the maximum upward adjacent score step along submitted rank order between faithful outputs is 5.27e-5 — which is why an exact-permutation rank check would reject correct outputs and the banded condition is used instead. Alignment must be by (cluster, gene); an earlier naive positional comparison of the differently ordered independent file produced a spurious 25.22 max |Δlog2fc| that the key-aligned comparison refutes at 1.9e-6.

**Controls — what they do and do not show (descriptions of the already-executed control suite).** The executed controls comprise valid cases (reference self-comparison, the recomputed oracle, the independent no-scanpy implementation, near-tie swaps with sub-zone zeroing, quoting/formatting variants, within-tolerance perturbations) and failure cases (missing/empty/malformed files, key misalignments, wrong-value variants, boundary probes on the tolerance band). Two limitations are now stated rather than implied: (i) several frozen probes mutate more than one aspect at once, so such a probe demonstrates that the combined artifact fails its named check — not that each constituent mutation is independently detected in isolation; (ii) probe branches whose perturbations sit inside a tolerance (e.g. a 5e-5 score perturbation passing against the 2e-4 band) demonstrate the boundary location and the tolerance's acceptance behaviour, not detection power for any error. No isolated per-mutation coverage is claimed, and no detection guarantee is inferred from no-op-like branches.

**Remaining limitations (unchanged by this revision).** (1) Every tolerance necessarily accepts differences below it: a genuine score error ≤2e-4, a log2fc error ≤1e-4, or a relative p-value error ≤1% passes undetected. (2) The small-tail zone treats any two values ≤1e-303 as equivalent regardless of ratio, so a wrong tail evaluation below the zone is indistinguishable from a correct one at that scale. (3) Rank consistency is adjacent-banded and tolerates cumulative inversions whose adjacent steps stay within the band. (4) The grader never re-derives the cell population or gene universe; conformity there is inferred from key coverage against the reference cross product, so a submission whose preprocessing deviates yet yields a coincidentally identical gene universe is indistinguishable by design. (5) Agreement evidence covers the reference self-comparison plus the two independently executed double-precision implementations on this dataset; no broader portability or software-coverage claim is made, and byte-identical reruns were used only as repeatability checks. (6) Reference labels, gene sets, per-cluster counts and numeric values are read dynamically from the reference file; no expected dimensions or answer values are embedded in the grader.
