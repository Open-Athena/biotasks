## Conclusions

Prioritize narrowly scoped SWE-smith/SWE-flow and portable historical correctness profiles that survive independent instruction, tolerance, alternative and incorrect-submission checks. These produced useful bounded library tasks, but often exercise software repair rather than independent scientific analysis. Independent review rejected plausible native exports: inadequate test coverage, an incorrect diagnosis, extra ungraded requirements and unjustified numerical precision. Native export success alone is insufficient for curation.

Keep PR-diff as an auxiliary source-edit similarity exercise across Python, C++ and R. Its deterministic reward with the optional model judge disabled grants substantial credit to reversed patch text in a pure scorer diagnostic; that string probe was not an executed incorrect solution. Neither fractional similarity nor a reward of one establishes functional correctness. Bare repository images and missing scientific dependencies also limit blind comparisons. Do not report a pooled success rate across these profiles and repeated lineages.

Free GLM can author and solve selected tasks, with explicit transport and reasoning-budget adaptations. The tested R2E and terminal-synthesis routes returned no native-valid exports, or incomplete captures/timeouts; expanding them requires a new, separately scoped experiment rather than repairing saved answers or resetting consumed allowances. Tasksmith/CodeMidas/FrontierSmith require explicit model-client work under the GLM-only constraint. TerminalWorld needs resolved recording/fixture terms; native equivalence needs a compatible caller environment and a separate reference-isolation decision. These findings apply to the pinned implementations and tested profiles, not universal impossibility.

Preserve canonical Harbor bundles plus source-specific Marin TaskSpec conversions. Six representative source examples across four structures completed 27 native/Marin reward case pairs. This does not certify every later runtime adaptation. Resolve SCALER's -1/+1 reward-range metadata, public immutable runtime images and verifier isolation before Atlas/training admission. **Atlas admissions: zero.** The permanent research branch and public evidence remain separate from any future promotion of supported tooling.
