# UCSC research cache supplement

These are unchanged research-review source snapshots, not a task release or
a build of UCSC software. Their copyright notices and upstream terms remain
applicable. BioTasks does not relicense these files.

- `source-review/2026-09-30/ucsc-README` is covered by the pinned repository
  root license, reproduced exactly in `licenses/kent.txt`.
- The two `ucsc-src-utils-*.c` files are covered by the explicit MIT license in
  `src/utils/LICENSE`, reproduced exactly in `licenses/kent-utils.txt`.
- `provenance.json` records source URLs, revision, blob identifiers, hashes
  and sizes. No other parts of the UCSC source tree are included.

The root license explains exceptions for other directories, including
liftOver. This supplement contains no liftOver source, executable binaries,
chain files, biological datasets or downloaded mixed directory/help pages.
The broader terms are documented at <https://genome.ucsc.edu/license/>.
