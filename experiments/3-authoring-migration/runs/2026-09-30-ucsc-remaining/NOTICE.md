# UCSC research captures

These three files preserve the original research-review cache, unchanged.
They retain their upstream copyright and terms; inclusion in this archive
does not place them under BioTasks' license or the utilities' MIT license.

- `ucsc-src-hg-liftOver-liftOver.c` is subject to the custom terms reproduced
  in `licenses/kent-liftover.txt`. They distinguish academic, nonprofit and
  personal use from commercial download and installation.
- `ucsc-linux-index.html` and `ucsc-linux-FOOTER.txt` are captured public
  directory/help pages containing usage text for multiple tools, including
  BLAT and liftOver. Their exact original bytes and source URLs are recorded
  in `provenance.json`. No blanket MIT license is asserted for these captures.
- The pinned root, utilities, liftOver and BLAT notices are included for
  context. They are not proof that both historical web captures correspond
  to that pinned source revision: the captures report different versions.
