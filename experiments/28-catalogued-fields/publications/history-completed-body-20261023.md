Assess official Repo2RLEnv `pr_runtime` across the same ten catalogued repositories' merged-PR history, without the September cutoff, using the pinned native core and only its built-in model calls. Discovery, budget and resource adaptations are checkpointed; no extra task/environment repair loop was used.

**Completed:** 83 exports, **74 positive tracked-test control pairs / 59 clean oracle commands**. Biology contributes 66/51; medical imaging contributes 8/8. The 23,285-PR inventory partitions into 1,961 entered, 100 upstream-discovery omissions and 21,224 backlog under the fixed runtime allocation. This was bounded validation, not an exhaustive suitability census.

**Actual OpenRouter cost:** $6.113565 history / $12.088851 including the separate September baseline, below the $100 ceiling. All 141 calls and 44.113263 allocated native CPU-hours reconcile. All experiment jobs are terminal; owned containers/images are cleaned up and the controller is stopped.

**Retention:** nine complete original public archives; Matchms has curated public evidence with per-file terms/attribution and untouched originals retained locally. Public-only Matchms replay is incomplete. No solver score, production-readiness claim or task/image dataset release.
