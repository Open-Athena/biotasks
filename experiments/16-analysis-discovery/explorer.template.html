Findings and limitations

Start Kaggle discovery from biology challenges. Competition pages connect relevant notebooks to named biological datasets. Package documentation and curated indexes provide complementary workflows.

Documents are not independent studies. PBMC3k, AdK trajectories and HMS challenge data recur across sources. Study clusters are recorded in document details; their labels have not been independently validated.

Input availability remains a constraint. Geneformer and DREAM examples include unresolved processed inputs or model assets. Two Kaggle leads have access attempts only. Some analytical examples lack an established biological specimen or contrast.

Coverage is incomplete. Systematic paper-to-code searches, citation following, other challenge platforms, and multilingual resources remain untested. No candidate has entered task authoring or execution.

How to read these results

Unit
One analysis document. Confirmed mirrors are aliases; sharing a dataset does not make two documents duplicates.
Screening
Apparently suitable: a biological question, meaningful analytical choices, specific input paths and identifiable dependencies. Excluded: fails these criteria. Unresolved: insufficient accessible evidence.
Data
Input locations were inspected, not downloaded and executed. Observed, adapted, predicted and toy/simulated inputs remain explicitly labeled in source details.
Selection
Purposive searches through GitHub, biology challenges on Kaggle, Bioconductor workflows and vignettes, tool documentation, galleries and curated indexes. Route totals combine these searches and are descriptive, not estimates of platform yield.
Taxonomy
Thirteen biology subdomains, with multiple labels permitted per document. Workflow labels describe the analysis separately. These annotations are not an independently validated ontology.
Source terms
Code, documentation, biological data and pretrained assets can have different terms. Index inclusion and static suitability do not establish reuse permission.
Reproduction
Manifest, search metadata, hashes and a standard-library builder live alongside this HTML on the research branch. Counts reproduce offline; remote discovery and rendered pages may change. Source bytes are not archived here.

Most source assessments are assistant judgments. Popularity signals are recorded where available but are not comparable across platforms. No corpus-wide recall, scientific score, or release-readiness claim follows from this pilot.
